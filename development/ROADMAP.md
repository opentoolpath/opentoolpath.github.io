# Proposed roadmap — agreement before editions

Status: unadopted planning proposal, 2026-09-10. S0–S4 are relative cycles, not promised
calendar releases. Start dates, owners and capacity require participant consent. This
roadmap is governed by [PROCESS.md](PROCESS.md); a season may finish without a release.

## Two workstreams in every season

**Agreement:** needs → alternatives → review → decisions → evidence → edition.
**Community:** explain → invite → onboard → contribute → show influence → return.

Neither is subordinate to PR throughput. Invite affected participants before decisions,
and publish useful learning even when a decision is deferred. Limit a season to three
technical decision packages; keep routine governance and public explanation lightweight.

| Cycle | Outcome sought | Decision packages and limits | Awareness and participation | Exit checkpoint |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S0 — joint foundation | Legitimate mandate and shared problem statement | Governance/roles, experiment separation and naming; initial use-case scope. No geometry/wire ratification | Joint invitation; one-page problem brief; introductory session; producer/consumer interviews; explicit gaps in representation | B0/B1 complete; roster/process ratified; at least 2 independent P and 2 C parties; S1 scope approved |
| S1 — common meaning | Agreement on what must be exchanged | DP-101 coordinates/units; DP-102 path/order/basic geometry boundaries; DP-103 process/orientation needs from real examples | Public worked examples for machining, robotics and AM simulation; first-contribution clinic; publish alternatives and unresolved needs | Each included DP has reviewed alternatives, named consumer need, recorded disposition and valid ballot; publish a reviewed draft only |
| S2 — first exchange contract | A compact candidate someone else can implement | DP-201 agreed JSON representation/versioning; DP-202 required/optional data and unsupported-input behavior; DP-203 references/package boundary only where needed | Independent producer/consumer demo; open review clinic; invite previously absent sectors; publish “what changed after your feedback” | 30-day public review, material-change rereview where needed; independent exchange and feature evidence; R gates if ready |
| S3 — use and consolidation | Learn from actual adoption before expansion | Fix demonstrated ambiguities; compatibility/migration; at most one optional-module candidate backed by use and maintenance ownership | Case-study session with consenting users; onboarding recap; invite reviewers who raised earlier concerns; share unresolved integration costs | Maintenance report and repeat engagement; stable edition only if all gates pass; no automatic binary/spline roadmap |
| S4 and recurring — revision | Confirm a useful core and identify the next narrow need | Reconfirm core; evaluate optional proposals; revise/deprecate only with evidence and transition plan | Annual open community review; rotate facilitation; mentor first-time editors; renew stakeholder coverage map | Confirm/amend/revise/withdraw decision; next-season scope and owners |

S0 may take several months; S1/S2 can span multiple nominal seasons. At most one ordinary
stable core edition per year is an initial planning limit, not a release deadline.
A review candidate does not imply that partners have committed to shipping support.

## Explicitly uncommitted options

Spline/NURBS, binary transport, streaming, formal proof infrastructure, generated registries,
digital signatures and certification have no scheduled adoption. Existing experiments can
inform a proposal, but each option must enter through a use case, alternatives and gates.
“No module needed” is an acceptable outcome. No requirement to adopt the existing SDK family.
