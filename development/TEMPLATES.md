# Minimal operating templates

Use only the sections needed for a real item. Markdown issues/documents are enough.
Do not open an empty bureaucracy for every code change. The authoritative decision rules
are in [PROCESS.md](PROCESS.md); these forms are aids, not substitute approvals.

## Use case and first contribution — UC-###

- Human contact and affiliation (publish with consent):
- Producer → consumer; what is exchanged and why:
- Small shareable input/example; expected information recovered:
- Current difficulty, lost information or integration cost:
- Required vs useful information; units/frame/process assumptions:
- What success would look like; what this example does not prove:
- Permission to publish the sample; private details removed:
- Acknowledged by / date / next response owner:

A newcomer need not propose a schema or implement an SDK to submit this form.

## Decision package — DP-###, revision n

- Title, season, human editor and constituency reviewers:
- Status; exact text/artifact commit or hash:
- UC references; mandate/scope decision:
- Proposed decision in plain language; proposed normative wording separately:
- Options A/B/deferral and comparison matrix:
- Hard requirements agreed before selection:
- Core/optional/deferred placement; dependencies and affected decisions:
- Compatibility in both directions; implementation and maintenance burden:
- Evidence for and against; independent reviewers and limitations:
- Rights/dependency issues; public introduction/example link:
- Comment/objection table; proposed dispositions:
- Review dates; requested rule; ballot reference:
- Outcome, rationale, dissent and release linkage (empty until decided):

## Comment and objection log — OB-###

| ID | DP/clause/version | Contributor | Technical/procedural question and evidence | Response owner | Disposition and rationale | Changed text | Contributor response / appeal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pending | — | — | — | — | — | — | — |

Record an unresolved evidence blocker separately from preference disagreement. Do not
mark an item “resolved” merely because the author stopped replying.

## Ballot and decision record — B-###

- Decision IDs and exact immutable text; dependencies:
- Process revision and rule; gate checklist:
- Frozen roster, affiliations/constituencies and eligibility date:
- Notice start/end; ballot start/end in UTC; any extension:
- Party-by-party positions, rationales and non-responses:
- Per-DP counts: N/Y/V/A/U; quorum; required Yes; P and C support:
- Objection dispositions and minority statement links:
- Outcome and arithmetic checked by two named parties:
- Appeal deadline, appeals and outcome; effective date if adopted:

## Release record — REL-###

- Status, edition identifier and source commit:
- Included DP IDs and excluded/deferred items:
- Exact artifact manifest: filename, SHA-256, status/version:
- Comment dispositions, evidence/coverage and independent exchange report:
- Bidirectional compatibility, migration/deprecation and known limits:
- Document/code license notices and rights review record:
- Changelog diff from previous adopted edition (or first-edition baseline):
- R ballot, independent verifier and appeal-window closure:
- Publication date/URL; predecessor/successor/advisory links:

## Public season invitation — draft text, not authorization to send

“We are exploring a shared toolpath exchange specification between independent producers
and consumers. The current work is [status], not a completed standard. This season asks
[three plain-language questions]. We welcome [affected applications], especially
[missing perspectives]. You can start with [worked example], submit a use case or comment
without becoming a voting member, or join [open session/asynchronous channel]. Review
closes [date]. Decisions and responses will be published at [location]. No implementation
or endorsement commitment is expected from attending.”

## Season close-out and community report

- What participants needed; who contributed (only with consent):
- Decisions reached, alternatives rejected and reasons:
- What changed because of external input; what remains uncertain:
- Deferred work, maintenance cost and next-season questions:
- New/returning contributors and independent constituency coverage:
- Feedback acknowledgment/disposition delays and onboarding lessons:
- One next action for a newcomer; next public review/learning opportunity:

Use honest maturity labels. Report unknown metrics as unknown; never invent adoption,
partners, votes, endorsements or external validation to complete a report.
