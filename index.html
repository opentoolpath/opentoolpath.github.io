Formally Verified & Mathematically Rigorous

OpenToolpath is backed by formal Lean 4 theorems and the non-negotiable principle of zero silent drops.

📐

Theorem I6 & I7: ZYZ Euler Invariance

Proves exact-real round-trip recovery of 5/6-axis unit tool vectors through ZYZ Euler angles, strictly independent of the free roll spin angle rz2.

⭕

Theorem I8: Arc Midpoint Sweep

Intermediate circular arc midpoint construction mathematically bisects the signed sweep and preserves the exact radius ρ, eliminating robot circular drift.

🔒

Byte-Accurate Container

PKZIP 2.04g archive with uncompressed 32-byte mimetype at byte offset 30 and SHA-256 cryptographic digests in manifest.json for instant file(1) detection and tamper protection.

🛡️

Refuse-Never-Clamp

Zero silent drops. Parsers and post-processors reject invalid or out-of-envelope moves with explicit diagnostic records rather than silently clamping coordinates.

⚡

Tiered Multi-Payload

Supports JSON text wire interchange (toolpath.json), Arrow columnar binary (toolpath.dry0), and FlatBuffers streaming binary (toolpath.dry1).

🌐

Multi-Domain Kinematics

Unifies subtractive 5-axis milling, industrial 6-axis robotics (ABB, KUKA, Fanuc, UR), additive FFF/DED, and laser cutting in a single schema.
