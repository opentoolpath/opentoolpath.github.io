A proposal for working together

Learn, review, decide.
Then revise.

The proposed process combines equal participation, visible decision records and recurring review cycles. Its membership rules, voting thresholds and timetable also need agreement. None has been ratified.

  1. Agree the starting scope

    Invite affected producers and consumers. Explain the problem, collect small examples and make missing perspectives visible before selecting a design.

  2. Review decisions in groups

    Use seasonal work packages, compare smaller alternatives and publish responses to objections. Formal decisions follow the agreed review process.

  3. Build evidence for an edition

    Look for independent exchanges, record compatibility and limitations, and publish stable editions only when their review gates are met.

Status update · 10 September 2026

What is available today?

Discussion drafts, not a standard release

This site is an entry point to scope and process discussions. There is no jointly adopted specification edition, ratified governing group or certification program announced here. The roadmap describes proposed work, not promised release dates.

Why the earlier demos and repository links changed

The earlier specification, SDKs, validation tools and integrations were developed independently as one possible approach. Those repositories now remain in the same organization but are private. The embedded viewer and downloadable experimental samples have been removed from this portal.

Earlier “v1.0”, “normative” and formal-verification wording overstated the shared project's maturity. The experiment is pre-alpha; its internal versions and tests are not evidence of partner approval or a completed standard.

How the community can help shape the next step

The proposal is to begin with open examples and an agreed scope, then use review sessions, documented decisions and revision cycles. New participants should be able to see how their feedback affects the work. No session dates, voting appointments or partner commitments are announced yet.
